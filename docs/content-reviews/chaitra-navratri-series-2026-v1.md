## 2. Textual Foundation & Scriptural Authority

In accordance with Shoonaya Rule 3 (Spiritual Content Integrity), this series is grounded in primary Shaktic literature:

1. **Devi Mahatmya (Durga Saptashati from the Markandeya Purana):**
   - The *Devi Kavacham* (Verses 3–5): The definitive scriptural enumeration of the Navadurga:
     *Prathamam Shailaputri cha dvitiyam Brahmacharini... Navamam Siddhidatri cha Navadurga prakirtitah.*
2. **Shiva Purana (Rudra Samhita, Parvati Khanda):**
   - Detailed accounts of Parvati's birth to Himavat and Mena, her sustained tapas for Shiva, and her manifestations.
3. **Devi Bhagavata Purana (Book 3, Chapters 26–27):**
   - Classical prescriptions for the Navaratra Vrata in Vasanta (Spring) and Sharada (Autumn), the ritual preparation of Kalashasthapana, and the contemplative mindset required of the devotee.
4. **Rashtriya Panchang (Saka 1948):**
   - Calendar indexing for Chaitra Shukla Pratipada (Vasanta Navaratri commencement).
