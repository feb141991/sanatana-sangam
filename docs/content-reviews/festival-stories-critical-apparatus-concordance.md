## 1. Textual Authority & Critical Apparatus Standards

In accordance with Shoonaya Rule 3 (*Spiritual Content Integrity*) and Rule 15 (*Scriptural Governance*), every festival story surfaced in the Shoonaya PWA and Native App carries a primary scriptural citation that has been textually collated against authoritative critical editions and historical recensions:

1. **Vedic, Upanishadic & Purāṇic Canon:**
   - *Veda Samshodhana Mandala (Pune)* and *Aufrecht* recensions for Vedic Saṃhitās.
   - *Eighteen Principal Upanishads* (eds. V.P. Limaye & R.D. Vadekar) and *Advaita Ashrama* for Upaniṣads.
   - *Critical Edition of the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa* (Oriental Institute, Baroda, eds. G.H. Bhatt & U.P. Shah).
   - *Critical Edition of the Mahābhārata and Harivaṃśa* (Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, BORI, Pune).
   - *All-India Kashiraj Trust (Varanasi)* and *Gita Press Gorakhpur* for Mahāpurāṇas and Upapurāṇas.
   - *Sri Vani Vilas Press (Srirangam)* and *Nirnaya Sagar Press (Mumbai)* for Adi Shankaracharya and classical Stotras.
   - *Chowkhamba Sanskrit Series Office (Varanasi)* for Ayurvedic classics (Caraka Saṃhitā, Suśruta Saṃhitā).

2. **Sikh Canon & Historical Chronicles:**
   - *Damdami Bir* standard 1430 Ang authorized recension published by the Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee (SGPC, Amritsar).
   - *Sri Dasam Granth Sahib* critical text edited by Bhai Randhir Singh and Punjabi University Patiala.
   - *Vāran Bhāī Gurdās Jī* (SGPC authorized edition).
   - *Sri Gur Sobhā* (Kavi Senapati, 1711 CE) and *Prachīn Panth Prakāsh* (Rattan Singh Bhangu).

3. **Jain Canonical Āgamas & Prakaranas:**
   - *Ṣaṭkhaṇḍāgama with Dhavalā Ṭīkā* (ed. Dr. Hiralal Jain).
   - *Tattvārtha Sūtra* of Acharya Umasvati (ed. Pandit Sukhlalji, Bharatiya Jnanpith).
   - *Jain Āgama Series* published by Agama Anushilana Samiti / Jain Vishva Bharati (Ladnun, ed. Acharya Mahapragya).
   - *Kalpa Sūtra* and *Sacred Books of the Jainas* (ed. Hermann Jacobi).

4. **Buddhist Tipitaka & Mahāyāna Canon:**
   - *Pali Text Society (PTS, Oxford)* critical editions for Dīgha Nikāya, Saṃyutta Nikāya, Aṅguttara Nikāya, Khuddakapāṭha, Dhammapada, and Vinaya Piṭaka (ed. T.W. Rhys Davids, Helmer Smith, V. Fausböll).
   - *Vipassana Research Institute (VRI, Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana)*.
   - *Buddhist Sanskrit Texts Series* (Mithila Institute, Darbhanga, ed. Dr. P.L. Vaidya) for Kāraṇḍavyūha Sūtra.
