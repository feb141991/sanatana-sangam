## 1. Scope & Observance Structure

Chhath Puja is an ancient, rigorous four-day Vedic-folk solar observance celebrated primarily in Bihar, Jharkhand, Eastern Uttar Pradesh, the Terai region of Nepal, and globally across the Dharmic diaspora. Commencing on Kartik Shukla Chaturthi and culminating on Kartik Shukla Saptami, the canonical series consists of 4 sequenced children:

|:---:|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | `chhath-nahay-khay` | Nahay Khay / नहाय-खाय / ਨਹਾਏ-ਖਾਏ | Purification and sanctification; sacred bath, thorough cleaning of the home, single satvik meal prepared with rock salt and ghee. |
| 2 | `chhath-kharna` | Kharna / खरना / ਖਰਨਾ | Full-day fast; evening offering of jaggery kheer and rotis (*Rasiya-Kheer*), initiating the arduous 36-hour waterless (*Nirjala*) fast. |
| 3 | `chhath-puja` | Sandhya Arghya / Chhath Puja / संध्या अर्घ्य / छठ पूजा / ਸੰਧਿਆ ਅਰਘ / ਛਠ ਪੂਜਾ | Offering Arghya to the setting Sun (*Pratyaksha Devata*) standing in waist-deep water with bamboo soop containing harvest offerings; worship of Chhathi Maiya. |
| 4 | `chhath-usha-arghya` | Usha Arghya / उषा अर्घ्य / ਊਸ਼ਾ ਅਰਘ | Offering Arghya to the rising Sun; prayer for health, vitality, and progeny; concluding the fast with sacred Prasad (*Paran*). |

## 2. Textual Foundation & Scriptural Authority

In accordance with Shoonaya Rule 3 (Spiritual Content Integrity), this series is grounded in authentic Vedic solar theology and Puranic literature:

1. **Rigveda (Surya Suktas):**
   - *Rigveda* 1.115.1: *Sūryo devīm uṣasaṁ rocamānām... Sūrya ātmā jagatas tasthuṣaś ca* ("The Sun is the living soul of all that moves and is motionless"). The worship of the visible divine embodiment of cosmic energy, light, and life.
2. **Devi Bhagavata Purana (Book 9, Chapter 46):**
   - Narrative of *Shashti Devi* (Devesena / Chhathi Maiya), the sixth manifestation of Prakriti: the goddess of progeny, child wellbeing, and moral protection. Honored alongside the Sun God on Shukla Shashthi.
3. **Mahabharata (Vana Parva, Chapter 3):**
   - The teaching of the 108 names of Surya by Sage Dhaumya to Yudhishthira, and Kunti's reverence for the solar deity, demonstrating the antiquity of disciplined solar sadhana during times of trial.
4. **Bhavishya Purana (Brahma Parva):**
   - Detailed treatises on Saura Dharma (solar worship), prescribing rigorous purity, non-injury, reverence for water reservoirs, and morning/evening twilight (*Sandhya*) offerings.

## 3. Cultural Integrity & Egalitarian Character

- **Priestless and Casteless Sanctity:** Chhath Puja is uniquely democratic and unmediated—the *Vratin* (practitioner) directly conducts the prayers and offerings at the waterbody without intermediate priestly hierarchies.
- **Reverence for Both Phases of Life:** Unlike most traditions that revere only the rising Sun, Chhath Puja distinctly begins its main worship with the setting Sun (*Sandhya Arghya*), celebrating completion, old age, and transition before saluting the dawn (*Usha Arghya*).
- **Zero Fabrication:** Refrains from inventing synthetic rules; preserves authentic vernacular terms (*Thekua*, *Daura*, *Soop*, *Ghat*) while presenting sublime Dharmic reflections on ecology, community, and gratitude.
