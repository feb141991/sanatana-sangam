## 2. Textual Foundation & Scriptural Authority

In accordance with Shoonaya Rule 3 (Spiritual Content Integrity), this series is grounded in verified Vedic, Puranic, and historical literature:

1. **Ganapati Atharvashirsha Upanishad (Atharva Veda):**
   - The foundational Vedic hymn of Ganesha: identifying Ganesha as the supreme reality (*Tvam eva pratyaksham tattvam asi*), creator, preserver, and dissolver (*Tvam eva kevalam kartha'si*), beyond the three gunas, states of consciousness, and bodies.
2. **Mudgala Purana & Ganesha Purana:**
   - Classical narratives of Ganesha's philosophical forms (Vakratunda, Ekadanta, Mahodara, Gajanana, Lambodara, Vikata, Vighnaraja, Dhumravarna), representing the subjugation of internal afflictions: Matsarya (envy), Mada (pride), Moha (delusion), Lobha (greed), Krodha (anger), Kama (lust), and Mamata (possessiveness).
3. **Bhavishya Purana (Uttara Parva, Chapter 31):**
   - The observance of *Anant Vrata* on Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturdashi: worship of the infinite Vishnu with the sacred 14-knot thread (*Ananta Sutra*), coinciding with the culmination of the 11-day Ganeshotsav.
4. **Padma Purana & Skanda Purana:**
   - Philosophical meaning of *Visarjan*: Divine presence invoked into earth and water (*Prithvi & Jala*), worshipped through love and discipline, and surrendered back into the unmanifest waters, reminding the seeker of the transient nature of all manifest forms.

## 3. Regional Flexibility & Ethical Invariants

- **Multi-Day Family Immersion Flexibility:** Recognizes that traditional households observe Visarjan at different durations (1.5 days, 3 days, 5 days, 7 days, or full 11 days on Anant Chaturdashi). Content guides practitioners according to their family custom without imposing a single compulsory day.
- **Ecological Sanctity:** Recommends traditional biodegradable clay (*Shadu mati*) and eco-friendly home immersions in accordance with Dharmic reverence for sacred water bodies (*Apas*).
- **Zero Fabrication:** Rigorously omits synthetic commercial superstitions, fabricated "luck" claims, and unauthorized modern accretions.
