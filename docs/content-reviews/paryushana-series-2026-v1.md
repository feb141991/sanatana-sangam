## 2. Textual Foundation & Scriptural Authority

In accordance with Shoonaya Rule 3 (Spiritual Content Integrity), this series is grounded in authoritative Jain canonical and philosophical literature:

1. **Kalpa Sutra (Chedasutras):**
   - Recitation of the *Kalpa Sutra* during Paryushana, describing the lives of the Tirthankaras (specifically Lord Rishabhanatha, Parshvanatha, Neminatha, and Mahavira Swami) and the rules for monastic and lay conduct during the rainy season retreat (*Varshavasa*).
2. **Tattvartha Sutra of Acharya Umasvati:**
   - Chapter 9 (Verses 1–6): The ten virtuous qualities (*Dashalakshana Dharma*): *Uttama Kshama* (Supreme Forgiveness), *Mardava* (Humility), *Arjava* (Straightforwardness), *Shaucha* (Purity), *Satya* (Truth), *Samyama* (Self-Restraint), *Tapa* (Austerity), *Tyaga* (Renunciation), *Akinchanya* (Non-Attachment), and *Brahmacharya* (Chastity).
3. **Acharanga Sutra:**
   - Foundational exposition of Ahimsa: all beings desire life, none wish to be harmed, injured, or enslaved.
4. **Pratikramana Sutra & Kshamavani:**
   - The classical Prakrit formula for universal forgiveness:
     > *Khāmemi savva-jīve, savve jīvā khamantu me;*  
     > *Metti me savva-bhūesu, veraṁ majjha na keṇavi.*  
     > ("I forgive all living beings; may all living beings forgive me. I cherish friendship toward all creatures; I hold malice toward none.")

## 3. Tradition Neutrality & Editorial Controls

- **Shvetambara & Digambara Harmony:** The series respectfully accounts for both major traditions (the 8-day Shvetambara Paryushana and the 10-day Digambara Dashalakshana Parva) without partisan claims of superiority or invalidation.
- **Living Observance Guidance:** Framing emphasizes personal restraint, mindfulness, and spiritual cleansing rather than enforcing rigid fasting edicts. Those unable to observe complete waterless fasting (*Nirjala Upavasa*) are guided toward *Samayika* (48 minutes of equanimity meditation), *Swadhyaya* (sacred study), and speech restraint (*Mauna*).
- **Zero Fabrication:** No invented ritual procedures, synthetic mantras, or speculative astrological results are presented.
