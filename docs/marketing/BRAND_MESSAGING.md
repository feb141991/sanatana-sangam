## 2. Core Pillars & Value Propositions

1. **Ad-Free & Sacred**: Zero banner ads, zero popups, zero commercial noise.
2. **Precision Astronomy**: Calculated from planetary ephemeris, Lahiri ayanamsha, and council review — not copied Gregorian charts.
3. **Heritage & Lineage (Kul)**: Private spaces to record family tithis, ancestor remembrance, and sacred memories.
4. **Stories That Shape Us**: Verified historical sakhis, puranic kathas, and Panchatantra morals.
5. **Cultural Play**: Classical Chausar (Gyan Chaupar / Leela) as an interactive spiritual contemplation tool.
6. **Unified Four Traditions**: Authentic reverence for Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, and Jain wisdom.
