# SGPC Lunar Anchor Computation

All dates below are **computed**, not independently verified. The method is
the corrected engine's sunrise scan at Amritsar (`31.634, 74.872,
Asia/Kolkata`) using the declared amanta lunar anchor and first qualifying
date. It is only used outside a covering printed SGPC edition; a printed
edition remains the provenance authority when present.

| Rule | Anchor | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 | 2030 | Max disagreement vs printed edition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| guru-nanak-gurpurab | Kartika Shukla 15 | 2027-11-14 | 2028-11-02 | 2029-11-20 | 2030-11-10 | not measured: no covering printed row |
| guru-gobind-singh-gurpurab | Pausha Shukla 7 | 2027-01-15 (SGPC 558) | 2028-01-04 | no Gregorian 2029 occurrence | 2030-01-10 | 0 days for the 2027 printed row; otherwise not measured |
| guru-tegh-bahadur-martyrdom | Margashirsha Shukla 5 | 2027-12-03 | 2028-11-21 | 2029-12-10 | 2030-11-29 | not measured: no covering printed row |

The 2029 Gobind result is absent because Pausha Shukla 7 falls in December
2028 and the next occurrence does not fall in Gregorian 2029. No fixed-table
date is substituted.

Holla Mohalla remains the separate Holi + 1 convention and is not converted
to a lunar anchor in this commit.
