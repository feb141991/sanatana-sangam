## 1. Scope and limits of this record

This record captures the founder's confirmation that a DPA / applicable
agreement has been executed with each of the 8 vendors. It does **not**
constitute:

- an engineering review of any signed document's actual content (SCC module
  used, IDTA text, destination-region specifics, sub-processor lists);
- a completed Transfer Risk Assessment (TIA) for any transfer — a TIA is a
  distinct legal analysis of transfer risk, not automatically satisfied by a
  DPA's existence;
- outside counsel review of DPA adequacy for this business's specific
  processing activities.

The register (`INTERNATIONAL_TRANSFERS_REGISTER.md`) has been updated to
reflect "DPA executed" per vendor, replacing the prior "missing" status, and
flags that per-vendor mechanism specifics still need verification against the
actual documents.
